Our Specialties

Expedited freight and container drayage.

Every mode is covered, but these two are where CTM Freight does its best work.

01

Expedited freight

When a line is down, a part is late or a shipment simply has to be there by morning, expedited is what we do best. We match the right-sized vehicle to the load so you are not paying for a 53' trailer to move two pallets, and we stay on the shipment from pickup until it is signed for.

Same-day and next-day moves, team runs, direct point-to-point with no terminals and no transfers.

Sprinter & Cargo Vans

Fast, nimble capacity for parts, small pallet counts and time-critical boxes.

Straight Trucks

Box trucks with liftgate options for mid-size loads that need speed and care.

Hot Shots

Flatbed hot shot capacity for equipment, machinery and urgent open-deck freight.

02

Container drayage

Containers do not wait, and neither do last free days. We move import and export containers between the port, the rail ramp and your door, and we keep you posted at every step from availability to empty return.

Based in Charleston with a strong drayage network across the Southeast, plus coverage at ports and rail ramps nationwide.

Import Containers

Pickup at the terminal or rail ramp as soon as your box is available, delivered to your warehouse or DC.

Export Containers

Empty pickup, delivery to your shipper for loading, and the loaded return to the port before cutoff.

Hot & Priority Boxes

Containers that have to move today to beat a last free day, a vessel cutoff or a line-down situation.
